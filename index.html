Privacy Policy

Track Twist: Train Home · Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DENGKE ZHANG ("we", "us") handles information when you play Track Twist: Train Home (the "Game"), a puzzle mini game distributed through the SUD OpenPaaS platform (the "Platform"). We designed the Game to work without collecting your personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

  • The Game has no account system. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.
  • We do not operate any game servers, and we do not receive, sell or share your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels cleared, stars, coins, unlocked environments and trains, check-in records, sound and language settings) is saved only in the local storage of your device. This data never leaves your device through us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling the Game from the Platform.

3. Advertising

The Game offers optional rewarded video ads (for example, "watch a video to triple your coins"). You choose when to watch an ad; a reward is given only after the video is completed. These ads are provided by the Platform and its advertising partners, which may automatically collect information such as device model, operating system, advertising identifier, IP address, approximate region and ad interactions, in order to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. This processing is governed by the Platform's and its partners' own privacy policies. Where required by law, you will be asked for consent, and you can limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example, "Limit Ad Tracking" or "Delete advertising ID").

4. Information processed by the Platform

The Game runs inside the SUD OpenPaaS runtime. The Platform may process technical information needed to run, update and secure the Game, and may receive anonymous gameplay statistics (such as level start and level complete events) to help us improve difficulty and stability. These statistics do not identify you. Please refer to the Platform's privacy policy for details on how it handles data.

5. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). Because the Game itself does not collect personal information, no such information is held by us. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Since we do not hold personal information about you, most requests can be fulfilled by clearing the Game's local data. For data processed by the Platform or its advertising partners, please use the controls they provide or contact them directly. You may also contact us for help with any request.

7. Security and retention

Local game data stays on your device until you delete it. We do not keep copies of it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to play the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
